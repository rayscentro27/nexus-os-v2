# Nexus Proactive Research and Department Advancement Standard V1

Research has three modes: `REQUESTED_RESEARCH`, `GOCLEAR_PROACTIVE`, and
`NEXUS_DEPARTMENTAL_PROACTIVE`. All use the existing Research scheduler,
work queue, Alpha bridge, and department handoff records.

Every proactive question persists `WHY_THIS_RESEARCH`, `TRIGGER`,
`DEPARTMENT`, `BUSINESS_OR_NEXUS`, `PARENT_GOAL`, `PROJECT`, `QUESTION`,
`EXPECTED_VALUE`, and `SOURCE_PLAN`. A qualified or unresolved result may
create one bounded next question with a material uncertainty; duplicate,
stale, low-value, and human-decision-only work is parked.

Routine monitored-source checks are not Alpha reviews. Explicit mission-linked
work is Alpha-eligible and must carry an owner/project target. Alpha remains
the only review path, and department routing remains downstream of Alpha.
Observed evidence is not a guarantee, no customer-facing or consequential
action is implied, and trading research remains paper-only.

Department charters are the source of truth in
`configs/research_department_charters.json`. TikTok/short-form commerce is a
bounded public-signal research foundation only: product, price, engagement,
supplier, margin, fulfillment, creative, and policy signals may be researched;
no account, inventory, ad, purchase, or publication is created by Research.
