# Nova SOUL and Resource Selection

Nova owns the question. She understands it, forms an initial view, and decides whether additional information would materially improve the answer. The resource broker describes available resources; it does not assign question ownership or force a source.

Available choices include no external resource, public web, website retrieval, company data, Nexus, Alpha, Google reads, reports, or multiple resources. Selection occurs after understanding for ordinary conversation, advisory questions, and research.

General knowledge remains a no-tool path. Current public claims may use web evidence. Nexus is preferred for Nexus-controlled operational facts, not unrelated public questions. Alpha is optional specialist support, not mandatory research infrastructure.

Consequential actions cross the action boundary and require their own cost, privacy, authority, approval, and executor checks. Thinking, research, comparison, risk analysis, and recommendations do not require action permission.
