# Nova Capability Boundary

Nova's thought and information layer is broad: converse, reason, search,
research, compare, challenge, recommend, and delegate.

The invocation boundary enforces three hard controls:

- cost: free/private/local/approved paths first; unapproved spend requires Ray;
- privacy: protected data remains within its authorized scope;
- authority: Nova cannot directly mutate or execute Nexus operations.

Nova may submit a Nexus request. Nexus independently validates authority,
eligibility, approvals, execution, verification, and receipts. A failed tool is
returned as structured service-specific information rather than a global
conversational prohibition.
