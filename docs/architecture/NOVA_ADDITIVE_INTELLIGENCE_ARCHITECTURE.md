# Nova Additive Intelligence Architecture

## Permanent doctrine

**ADDING A TOOL OR KNOWLEDGE SOURCE MUST EXPAND NOVA'S CAPABILITY, NEVER REDUCE HER BASELINE INTELLIGENCE.**

**INTELLIGENCE IS BROAD. CONSEQUENCES ARE GOVERNED.**

Nova owns the question. Resources help her answer it; no resource owns the question or becomes a universal source of truth.

## Base intelligence

Nova’s existing conversational core provides general knowledge, conversation, interpretation, explanation, strategy, disagreement, and business-partner judgment. The runtime remains five stages: `pre_model_boundary`, `build_context`, `generate_response`, `validate_output`, and `compose_output`.

## Knowledge resources

Resources are descriptors, not pre-model routers. Each describes its domain, read capability, availability class, freshness, provenance, cost, privacy scope, retrieval method, and whether its content can be compared. Current resources include general model knowledge, public web/search, public websites, company data, Nexus OS, Nexus live truth, Alpha, granular Google Workspace reads, and report artifacts.

The broker is descriptive-only. Nova may choose no resource, one resource, or multiple resources after understanding the request. Adding a descriptor preserves every existing descriptor.

## Reasoning abilities

`UNDERSTAND`, `EXPLAIN`, `COMPARE`, `CHALLENGE`, `SYNTHESIZE`, `ECONOMIC_ANALYSIS`, `RISK_ANALYSIS`, `RECOMMEND`, `PLAN`, and `PRIORITIZE` are optional intellectual operations. They are not permission gates and do not authorize external effects.

## Action capabilities

Consequential actions cross one granular action boundary. The contract records side effect, authority, approval, cost, reversibility, and executor. Nova may think, research, draft, model, recommend, and prepare without crossing that boundary. Nexus remains the executor for governed Nexus work.

## Truth, memory, risk, and cost

- Truth validates claims using the source appropriate to the domain; it does not decide which questions Nova may consider.
- Memory preserves conversation and durable context; volatile capability, authentication, health, and runtime facts are queried live.
- Risk produces analysis, quantification, mitigation, testing, and measurement—not automatic prohibition.
- Resource selection prefers free, local, private, and approved paths. Unapproved spend remains blocked at invocation.

## Failure isolation

A failed resource returns a bounded failure to Nova. It does not erase base reasoning or make unrelated resources unavailable. Nova may try another authorized resource, use sufficient existing knowledge with uncertainty, ask for approval where cost requires it, or explain the specific remaining limitation.
