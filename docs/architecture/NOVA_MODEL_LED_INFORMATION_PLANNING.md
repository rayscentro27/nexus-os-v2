# Nova Model-Led Information Planning

For normal conversation, advisory questions, strategy, economics, public
research, and website analysis, Nova first understands Ray's goal and forms an
initial view. The information plan then identifies potentially useful
resources; it does not force a tool or source before reasoning.

Factual company questions may still use deterministic current reads. Explicit
commands, approvals, and delegation remain governed before execution.

The plan distinguishes general reasoning, public evidence, company data, Alpha,
Nexus truth, and artifact reads. Evidence is retrieved only through allowlisted
capabilities and is validated before a consequential claim is presented.
