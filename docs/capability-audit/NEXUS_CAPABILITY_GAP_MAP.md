# Nexus Capability Gap Map

## Core gaps

1. **Canonical worker plane:** local launchd is proven, but remote CPU/GPU jobs
   need a queue, capability adapter, normalized result, receipt, cancellation,
   and tenant boundary.
2. **Evidence ingestion at scale:** current research tools work, but a bounded
   browser/crawler and document-conversion lane is not yet canonical.
3. **Knowledge consolidation:** memory, reports, approved knowledge, and local
   indexes exist in several forms. A graph/retrieval enhancement must not become
   a second truth source.
4. **Multi-business configuration:** GoClear-specific configuration must be
   separated from reusable business identity, offers, policies, workflows,
   departments, and integration declarations.
5. **Trading research depth:** current Oanda/demo paths are adequate for safe
   Forex practice. Options chain history, assignment/exercise, Greeks, and
   portfolio risk are missing.
6. **Human media interface:** STT/TTS/voice/avatar/meeting components are
   scaffolds, not activated capabilities.

## Privacy gap

Client credit reports, financial records, private client documents, credentials,
and internal business records are **LOCAL_REQUIRED** or **LOCAL_PREFERRED**
until a tenant-aware encrypted worker plane and explicit cloud policy exist.
Public research and anonymized artifacts may be **CLOUD_ACCEPTABLE**. GPU
creative workloads are generally **CLOUD_PREFERRED** when they contain no
restricted source material.

## Product gap

At 10 businesses, the main bottleneck is not model intelligence; it is tenant
isolation, per-tenant provenance, cost allocation, secret scoping, worker
quotas, and supportable receipts. At 100 businesses, remote workers,
multi-tenant durable state, fleet observability, and provider portability are
mandatory. The Mac Mini must become a founder/private control node, not the
commercial fleet.
