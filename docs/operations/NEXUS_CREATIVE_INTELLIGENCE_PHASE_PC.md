# Nexus Creative Intelligence — Phase P-C

Phase P-C extends the existing Creative Lab and Creative Studio. The original Lab remains the deterministic/reference regression surface; its fixed `Evidence Ledger`, `Scout Brief`, and `Control Tower` territories are preserved as reference territories, not treated as the complete idea universe.

## Canonical state

The intelligence layer uses compact governed JSONL collections:

- `creative_concepts`: `nexus.creative-concept.v1`, semantic signatures, scores, provenance, and round metadata.
- `creative_feedback`: `nexus.creative-feedback.v1`, explicit review decisions and tags.
- `creative_preference_profiles`: `nexus.creative-preference-profile.v1`, derived only from recorded feedback.
- `creative_campaigns`: `nexus.creative-campaign.v1`, one selected concept translated across landing, Remotion, and image directions.

No vector database, media duplication, scheduler, approval store, or publishing system was added.

## Diversity and memory

Each serious round creates six concept-level directions spanning diagnosis, evidence/editorial, metaphor, progress system, premium restraint, and documentary utility. Each concept has normalized strategic angle, metaphor, narrative, emotion, hook, visual family, layout family, CTA, audience tension, and format fields.

Similarity is a weighted deterministic comparison of those dimensions plus central-idea token overlap. Recent history is compared most strongly. `LOW`, `MODERATE`, `HIGH`, and `NEAR_DUPLICATE` classifications are exposed. Cosmetic clones and high-similarity sets fail closed as `CREATIVE_DIVERSITY_INSUFFICIENT` within bounded generation; no infinite regeneration loop exists.

Novelty is kept separate from brief fit, brand fit, evidence safety, emotional strength, visual potential, conversion hypothesis, and production feasibility. GPU is not required.

## Preference learning

Only explicit feedback updates preference state. Positive and negative tags are tracked independently, so “love the metaphor” and “too much text” do not collapse into one winner/loser label. Synthetic certification feedback is labeled `synthetic_fixture=true` and is not presented as genuine Ray history.

Preference fit adjusts ranking but does not remove exploratory concepts or clone a prior winner. Audience/business performance remains separate; when Revenue Hub or analytics sources are unavailable, performance remains `UNKNOWN`.

## Cross-asset translation and governance

The selected concept preserves one central idea while adapting execution for landing-page structure, medium-specific Remotion story/rhythm, and ComfyUI-ready image metaphor/composition/prompt. All output is internal planning metadata with `external_action_performed=false`; public use remains subject to existing Ray approval. Remotion remains certified and ComfyUI remains capacity-deferred from Phase P-B.

## Live certification

The GoClear funding-readiness brief produced six distinct concepts, maximum within-set similarity `0.106`, and two exploratory directions. A synthetic fixture recorded `LOVE_METAPHOR` and `TOO_MUCH_TEXT`; the preference profile learned those dimensions independently, and a second round retained exploration while applying preference fit. A selected concept produced landing, video, and image directions with the same central idea and different medium execution.
