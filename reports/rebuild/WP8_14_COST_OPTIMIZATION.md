# WP8.14 Cost Optimization

Recommendations are advisory and consider cost, quality, reliability, latency, privacy, capability, migration burden, engineering burden, and business impact. `CHEAPER`, `FREE`, and `EXPENSIVE` are not decisions by themselves. Alpha may research alternatives and Jax may review technical migration risk; Finance combines those findings with actual usage before recommending KEEP, TEST_ALTERNATIVE, MIGRATE, REDUCE_USAGE, RENEGOTIATE, CANCEL_CANDIDATE, or NO_CHANGE.
