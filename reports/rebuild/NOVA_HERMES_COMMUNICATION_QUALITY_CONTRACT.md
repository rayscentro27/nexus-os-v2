# Nova Hermes Communication Quality Contract

Normal Telegram conversation must answer first, recommend early when asked, explain the two to four reasons that matter most, state meaningful uncertainty, and offer a next move only when useful.

The model-facing contract now explicitly requests conversational paragraphs, short bullets, no wide tables, no raw provenance metadata, and no schema headings such as `VERIFIED`, `BLOCKERS`, `RECOMMENDATION`, or `NEXT ACTION` for ordinary conversation. Report/audit/status/certification/breakdown/formal-comparison requests retain structured formatting. Evidence and provenance language remains unchanged in substance.

Direct recommendation remains required for choice/opinion prompts. Unsupported current or verified claims remain disallowed.
