# WP8.4 Legacy Concept Benchmark

The adopted concept was the legacy/Vibe-style chronological evaluation split
plus explicit failure classification and immutable journal evidence. Nexus
implemented the benchmark without importing legacy execution code.

Result: the concept improved evaluation visibility but did not improve trading
performance; the candidate failed held-out validation. Decision:
`ADOPT_CONCEPT` for evaluation structure, `KEEP_NEXUS` for execution/control.
