# WP8.7 Research Theme Registry

## Themes

TRADING, BUSINESS, MARKETING, and AI_NEXUS are persisted with bounded terms and LAST_30_DAYS as the default window. Themes are organizational research desks, not independent agents.
## Cycle state

AlphaDiscoveryLoop semantics: DISCOVERED → SCREENED → TRANSCRIPT/RETRIEVAL_PENDING → CLAIMS_EXTRACTED → VERIFYING → CHALLENGED → ROUTED/REJECTED/ARCHIVED.
