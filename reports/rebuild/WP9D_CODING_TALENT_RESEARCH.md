# WP9D coding-talent research

Current primary project pages were reviewed for OpenCode, Aider, Cline CLI, and
OpenHands; SWE-agent and Continue remain candidates requiring separate runtime
validation. The sources establish materially different architectures and
licenses, but do not prove local compatibility, security, or Nexus hiring.

- [OpenCode official repository](https://github.com/sst/opencode): MIT,
  provider-agnostic client/server/TUI orientation.
- [Aider official repository](https://github.com/Aider-AI/aider): Apache-2.0,
  terminal pair-programming CLI with multiple model routes.
- [Cline official repository](https://github.com/cline/cline): Apache-2.0,
  CLI/SDK/MCP/headless and approval-oriented workflow.
- [OpenHands official repository](https://github.com/OpenHands/OpenHands):
  SDK/CLI agent platform with heavier sandbox/model requirements.

Open source was not treated as zero operating cost. No paid model or runtime
dependency was introduced.
