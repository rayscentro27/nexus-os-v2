# Google and Nexus multi-resource proof

Hermes-native probes confirmed both mixed-resource surfaces without adding a
router or changing Nova behavior.

`Based on what Nexus says needs attention and what is on my calendar today,
what should I prioritize?` selected Nexus blocker/work-item reads and Google
Calendar search. The response synthesized the queued Voice browser-transport
work item with the empty Calendar result.

`Do any recent emails appear related to what Nexus currently says needs my
attention?` selected Nexus review reads and Gmail search. The response stated
that there were no pending Nexus reviews and separately described bounded email
evidence as potentially relevant; it did not treat subject similarity as proof
of a business relationship.

Both resource families retained their own provenance and freshness envelopes.
Google content was not forwarded to Web or Alpha. No raw Google API schema was
presented to the user. A repeated same-turn Gmail search appeared in the model
exchange; this is recorded for a later deduplication pass and does not alter
the current read-only capability boundary.
