## Intent Resolution

`resolve_intent()` now distinguishes `STATUS_REQUEST`, `PRIORITY_REQUEST`,
`CURRENT_REVIEW_REQUEST`, `DECISION_REQUEST`, `COMPARISON_REQUEST`,
`CASUAL_CONVERSATION`, and related gears. Each carries an expected output so a
priority request cannot silently degrade into a status response.

## Daily Priority Repair

The Oracle adapter sends a bounded priority contract requiring one primary
company/objective focus, why it ranks first, outcome advanced, Nexus next work,
and any actual Ray decision. A detected status/approval-shaped draft receives
one tool-disabled correction pass. The live production-equivalent result
selected the stalled Voice Repair/VOICE-001 work as the primary focus and did
not require Ray to authorize internal continuation.

## Priority Ranking

The ranking contract considers parent-goal importance, customer/economic
impact, dependency, urgency, learning value, resource availability,
reversibility, confidence, and Ray dependency. Newest task, loudest error,
available department, or degraded telemetry is not sufficient by itself.

## Evidence Relevance

The planning contract now requires each material fact to affect option ranking,
risk, uncertainty, or next action. Runtime health, model/provider status, and
telemetry are classified as context-only for pricing unless a causal mechanism
is established. Customer, competitor, willingness-to-pay, conversion,
fulfillment cost, margin, retention, and downstream economics are the relevant
pricing evidence classes.

## Irrelevant Evidence Suppression

The Oracle prompt explicitly suppresses status dumps and irrelevant technical
metadata in business decisions. The pricing correction pass removes degraded
telemetry and missing-client counts as direct pricing evidence while retaining
the valid statement that market/customer evidence is not yet sufficient.

## Decision Sufficiency

`decision_sufficiency()` distinguishes a final decision from a provisional
recommendation and a bounded-test state. Missing evidence does not become a
negative result. A parent decision remains open until its evidence or outcome
contract is satisfied.

## Provisional Recommendations

The pricing canary now states that `$97` is an unvalidated hypothesis, gives a
provisional direction, preserves alternatives, identifies missing evidence,
and recommends a bounded internal research/test path. It does not claim that
free pricing is permanently superior.

## Strategic Option Expansion

The pricing guidance preserves the requested `$97` versus free comparison while
allowing a materially useful third structure: free basic assessment plus paid
premium assessment, with low-cost/tiered models available for testing.

## Parent Decision Reframing

Nova is instructed to answer the immediate pricing question while recognizing
the deeper acquisition, customer-value, and lifetime-economics decision.

## Contradiction Judgment

Existing controlled contradiction coverage remains. The R1.2 relevance rules
add the missing test: specialist positions may disagree, but irrelevant system
telemetry cannot be used as a tie-breaker. The required real-user quality gate
will inspect whether this survives Telegram presentation.

## System Health vs Business Health

The prompt and planning contract now explicitly separate system operation from
customer acquisition, pricing, revenue, retention, and economics. A degraded
system read cannot imply that GoClear should lower its price without a causal
link.

## No-Data Reasoning

No clients, conversions, or pricing experiments are treated as unknown,
not-measured, insufficient-sample, or verified-zero only when the source
supports that distinction. No missing business evidence is converted into a
negative pricing conclusion.

## Casual Conversation

casual canary returned a natural greeting without executive headings or
specialist calls. Opinion conversation remained conversational, though its
genericity remains a quality-monitoring item.

light; current-state requests use an authoritative read; priority/decision
