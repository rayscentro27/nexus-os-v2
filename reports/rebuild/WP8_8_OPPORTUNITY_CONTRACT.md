The contract stores source research/claims, customer/problem, offer, market and competitor evidence, economics, risks, unknowns, feasibility, Nexus fit, automation potential, score, Nova decision, state, and timestamps.

Supported source types include ALPHA_DISCOVERY, NOVA_IDEA, RAY_IDEA, CLIENT_PATTERN, SEO_SIGNAL, FORUM_PROBLEM, YOUTUBE_IDEA, GITHUB_CAPABILITY, INTERNAL_NEXUS_LEARNING, PARTNERSHIP, AFFILIATE_PROGRAM, and MARKET_CHANGE.
