# Creative Visual QA

Preview: `public/marketing-previews/wp93-commercial-readiness.html`.

Real Playwright renders were captured at 1440px desktop and 390px mobile.
Both rendered the intended title, CTA, three-step structure, disclosure, and
had `scrollWidth <= viewport width=true`. Visual inspection found clear first
impression, legible hierarchy, visible CTA, responsive card collapse, and no
overflow. The visual language is restrained and educational rather than a
generic claims-heavy ad. No fake testimonial, logo, ranking promise, or
unsupported performance claim is present.

The preview is intentionally an internal draft and does not constitute real
market validation. Screenshot captures were used for this QA pass; no public
deployment occurred.
