# WP8.12 Operator Design System

`src/operator/operator.css` establishes the internal console primitives: dark operational sidebar, compact top bar, typography/eyebrow hierarchy, status pills, attention cards, panels, empty/degraded states, primary actions, responsive navigation, and mobile layout. Product UI Director concerns are encoded as workflow hierarchy and progressive disclosure; Design System Guardian concerns are centralized tokens/classes rather than per-page styling.

The actual screenshots show a calm, information-dense operating surface rather than a database schema or marketing page. The first visual critique found no blocking overflow or hierarchy defect after the authenticated rerender.
