# Truth and authority boundary

Hermes/Nova owns conversation, semantic selection, reasoning, and synthesis. Nexus owns operational truth, permissions, tenant isolation, authority, execution, receipts, and recovery. MCP is an interface only.

`MCP_DOES_NOT_GRANT_AUTHORITY=YES` and `READ_ONLY_MCP=YES`. No email, publish, payment, client mutation, trade, campaign launch, or other external write is exposed. Future mutation tools must call existing Nexus governance internally.
