# WP9E coding-talent research

Official project sources were checked for OpenCode, Aider, Cline, OpenHands,
SWE-agent and Continue. The installed candidate evidence in this environment
is limited to OpenCode; `continue` resolves to a shell command rather than a
verified coding-agent executable. The remaining candidates are absent.

OpenCode is MIT and provider-agnostic; Aider is Apache-2.0 and terminal based;
Cline CLI is Apache-2.0 with headless CLI/SDK and approval controls; OpenHands
and SWE-agent are heavier agent/benchmark stacks. Continue's own repository
now says it is no longer actively maintained and describes a final 2.0.0
release, so it is not a preferred trial candidate. Compatibility claims remain
candidate research until identical isolated tasks execute.

Sources: [Hermes](https://github.com/NousResearch/hermes-agent),
[OpenCode](https://github.com/sst/opencode), [Aider](https://github.com/Aider-AI/aider),
[Cline](https://github.com/cline/cline), [OpenHands](https://github.com/OpenHands/OpenHands),
[SWE-agent](https://github.com/SWE-agent/SWE-agent), [Continue](https://github.com/continuedev/continue).
