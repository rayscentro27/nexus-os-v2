# Nova SOUL Restriction Reconciliation

Baseline: `a742b8d`  
Implementation commit: `db8a025`

The previous SOUL was long and mixed identity, current truth rules, capability descriptions, and action restrictions. Its active value was replaced with a concise positive role definition.

## Result

- Broad conversation and reasoning preserved.
- Resource use is permitted when useful; resources do not own questions.
- Live capability state is required for volatile availability claims.
- Memory and old reports are not current truth.
- Cost, privacy, and Nexus authority remain enforced at consequence boundaries.
- Nova may prepare and submit bounded requests but may not bypass governed execution.

No new router or runtime stage was added. The remaining granular capability state is supplied by the existing broker and action contracts.
