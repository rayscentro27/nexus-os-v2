# Nova Resource Additivity Contract

Adding a resource must not reduce Nova's general intelligence.

- Nova owns the question and conversational voice.
- Resources provide evidence only when semantically useful or requested.
- Nexus owns canonical internal truth, authority, and execution.
- Web owns retrieved public information.
- Alpha owns specialist research results.
- Session history owns continuity, not present operational truth.
- Historical data remains available for historical questions and analysis.
- A resource-backed answer may identify a referent, but cannot establish current
  facts on a later turn.

This contract is intended to apply equally to future Google MCP and specialists.
