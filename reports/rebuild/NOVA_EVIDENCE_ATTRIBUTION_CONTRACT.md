# Nova Evidence Attribution Contract

Internal attribution distinguishes `VERIFIED_EXTERNAL_FACT`, `VERIFIED_NEXUS_STATE`, `SUPPORTED_ALPHA_FINDING`, `MODEL_JUDGMENT`, `ASPIRATIONAL_TARGET`, `HYPOTHESIS`, and `UNKNOWN`.

The final presentation remains Nova-owned prose. Numeric targets without direct support are recorded as aspirational/model judgment; they cannot be phrased as evidence-backed revenue. Currentness language requires current retrieved evidence or is rewritten as qualified judgment.
