# Current versus history contract

Conversation history is retained for referents and historical explanation. It
is not authoritative for present operational facts.

Precedence:

`FRESH_CANONICAL_NEXUS_STATE > PRIOR_TOOL_RESULT > PRIOR_ASSISTANT_CLAIM`

The direct resolver continues to exclude historical and synthetic opportunity
records and historical daily-brief blockers. Empty current results are valid.
The model may discuss historical records only when clearly labeled as history.
