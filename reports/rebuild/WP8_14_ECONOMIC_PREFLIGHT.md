# WP8.14 Economic Preflight

`campaign_preflight` produces downside/base/upside scenarios, upfront/continuous/max validation cost, price and variable cost, contribution and break-even. Unknown commercial inputs remain unknown. `trading_preflight` subtracts explicit spread, commission, slippage, and financing from gross expectancy and reports insufficient data when sample/expectancy is inadequate.

Budget checks return `PAUSE_OPTIONAL_CONSUMPTION`, preserve state, and escalate to Finance plus the owner; they do not mark the initiative failed.
