# Open Source Trading Concept Harvest

Approved concepts: time-series train/validation/held-out splits; next-bar execution; explicit spread/fee/slippage; event-driven state transitions; walk-forward capability; parameter-stability regions; portfolio exposure limits; immutable experiment journals; failure classification; and deterministic metric aggregation. These are mapped to Nexus experiment generation, trading contracts, journal/learning collections, and replay—not copied wholesale. Strategy examples remain hypotheses, never evidence.
