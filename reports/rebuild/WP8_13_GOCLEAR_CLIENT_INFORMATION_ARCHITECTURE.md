# GoClear Client Information Architecture

Canonical first journey: `Dashboard → Credit Review / Documents → Business Foundation → Funding Readiness → Funding Access / Request Review`. The V2 shell is a client-facing experience, separate from `/operator` and `/admin`. Funding Readiness is the endpoint and exposes one dominant next action, outstanding requirements, pillar status, Hermes context, and inline evidence upload.

Empty, loading, demo, live, provider-waiting, and blocker states are explicit; no UI-only completion is presented as client evidence.
