# WP8.3 Mobile Detailing Economics

The deterministic Python model is stored in the metrics collection, with
explicit source-backed versus model/test assumptions. Scenarios:

| Scenario | Monthly revenue | Gross margin | Break-even jobs/month |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Conservative | $4,800 | 79.2% | 31 |
| Base | $10,890 | 81.8% | 24 |
| Upside | $20,160 | 81.9% | 22 |

The base scenario uses 66 jobs/month, $165 average ticket, $30 variable cost,
$35 labor, $10 travel, $1,200 fixed, and $900 marketing assumptions. These are
not researched facts. Contribution margin is 54.55%; CAC payback is explicitly
`UNPROVEN`.

Membership structures modeled: monthly maintenance ($99), biweekly ($179),
multi-vehicle household ($299), and fleet/business ($250). Frequency consumes
capacity; route density, retention, and discount burden require validation.
