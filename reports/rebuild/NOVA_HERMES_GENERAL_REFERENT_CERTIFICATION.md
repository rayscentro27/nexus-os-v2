# Nova Hermes general referent certification

A single shadow session ran the five-turn sequence: three sub-$1,000 business
ideas; “I like the second one”; why it could work; argue against it; would you
still choose it.

The post-guidance run preserved the selected idea in the conversation context,
returned a counterargument, and answered the final keep/change question without
an unrelated historical result. The shadow sidecar now retains a bounded recent
conversation index plus the active recommendation, while volatile resource
results are marked stale at the start of a new turn.

`SECOND_ONE_REFERENT=preserved`; `COUNTERARGUMENT=available`;
`FINAL_RECOMMENDATION=keep`, with the caveat that the model may change the
choice when new evidence warrants it.
