# WP8.14 After-hours Finance Contract

Before a nightly loop: check cash, known credit/quota, compute, storage, provider health, and an approved envelope. Afterward: ingest immutable work-order/resource/cost receipts, roll up by department and initiative, compute forecast variance, and produce the daily ledger/Nova CFO brief. Authority remains internal-only; Finance cannot publish, spend, pay, subscribe, or fund live trading.
