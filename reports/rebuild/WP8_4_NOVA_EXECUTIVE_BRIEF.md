# WP8.4 Nova Executive Brief

We tested a canonical EUR/USD H1 SMA10/SMA30 crossover derived from the
existing scanner observation. In-sample results were positive but validation
and held-out results were negative, cost stress stayed negative, and trade count
was too small. The strategy was rejected for paper observation.

Nexus learned that this candidate requires a different hypothesis or stronger
evidence. Ray does not need to approve a paper run for this rejected version;
the next decision is whether to authorize a future candidate research cycle.
