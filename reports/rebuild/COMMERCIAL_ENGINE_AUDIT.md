# Commercial Engine Audit

Alpha's WP9 dispatch previously invoked Creative's overnight queue generator
and Growth's no-spend validation loop independently. Creative returned an
internal draft/report artifact; Growth persisted a validation plan with zero
external exposure. Neither path consumed a specific Alpha claim, carried
campaign lineage, or selected a commercial next action.

This campaign adds a bounded mission builder that consumes a persisted Alpha
claim, creates a Finance-labeled validation-ready brief, three materially
different territories, a landing preview, channel-native drafts, disclosure,
and affiliate candidates. It does not publish or spend.

Authority remains `MAX_NEW_PAID_COST_USD=0`; public posting, paid ads,
outreach, payments, and customer mutations remain disabled.
