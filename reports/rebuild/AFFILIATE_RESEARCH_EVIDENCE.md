# Affiliate Research Evidence

Three partner candidates were checked against direct company/help sources.
Mercury and Relay pages were retrieved successfully; Bluevine's direct page
was unavailable to the bounded runtime and remains `UNKNOWN`, not endorsed.

Mercury's official accountant page advertises referral bonuses up to $1,500
per eligible client, while the official referral agreement states that exact
terms are governed by the partner agreement. Relay's official help material
states that its partner program is for accounting/bookkeeping/advisory firms,
requires eligible new U.S. businesses, and uses a partner portal or referral
link. These details are source observations, not an activated offer.

Sources:

- [Mercury for Accountants](https://mercury.com/for/accountants)
- [Mercury Referral Partner Agreement](https://mercury.com/legal/referral-partners)
- [Relay partner onboarding](https://relayfi.com/hc/en-us/articles/23382126147988-Onboarding-your-clients-to-Relay/)

Persisted candidate records include source URL, retrieval status/hash,
audience, fit, commission/terms as UNKNOWN where not directly established,
restrictions requiring review, confidence, and `No referral link activated`.
No affiliate revenue or conversion is claimed.
