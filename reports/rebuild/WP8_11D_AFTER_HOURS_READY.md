# WP8.11D After-hours Readiness

The storage and review route is ready for internal artifacts, but model-powered
after-hours operation is not certified because no model route completed an
inference. Therefore `CREATIVE_AFTER_HOURS_STORAGE_READY=NO` for the full
contract and `CREATIVE_CORE_AFTER_HOURS_READY=NO_MODEL_ROUTE_NOT_PROVEN`.

Once Ray restores a functioning approved model route, the existing bounded
runner may use: `MAX_BRIEFS=2`, `MAX_TERRITORIES_PER_BRIEF=4`,
`MAX_LANDING_PAGES=1`, `MAX_IMAGES=4`, `MAX_VIDEO_RENDERS=1`,
`MAX_AVATAR_RENDERS=1`, `MAX_AI_CALLS=12`, `MAX_CRITIQUE_CYCLES=2`,
`MAX_REVISIONS=1`, `MAX_API_COST_USD=0`, `INTERNAL_ONLY`, `NO_PUBLISH`,
`NO_AD_SPEND`, `NO_SOCIAL_POSTING`, `NO_OUTREACH`, and `NO_SUBSCRIPTIONS`.
