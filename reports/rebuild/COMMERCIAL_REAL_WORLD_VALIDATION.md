# Commercial Real-World Validation

`COMMERCIAL_EXTERNAL_AUTHORITY_AUDIT=PASS`: the repository contains draft and
organic/no-spend preparation paths, but no independently proven authorization
for public posting, paid advertising, outreach, or partner-link activation was
found in this bounded audit.

Accordingly the mission stops at:

`REAL_VALIDATION_READY=YES`

and

`COMMERCIAL_REAL_WORLD_CERTIFIED=NOT_PROVEN_AUTHORITY_GATED`.

The package defines real metrics, a zero-cash envelope, resource limits, a
sample/window to be configured only after approval, and stopping rules. It
contains no synthetic leads or invented traffic, revenue, CAC, EPC, or
commission.
