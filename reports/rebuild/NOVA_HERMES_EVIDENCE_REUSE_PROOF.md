# Hermes Nova evidence reuse proof

Resource requirements are current-turn scoped, while valid follow-ups may reuse
correlated evidence. A Research-result follow-up is not forced to rerun Alpha
when a fresh linked result exists. Conversely, an explicit new Nexus/current
outside-information objective cannot be satisfied by generic conversation
memory.

The contract distinguishes `required_resources`, `executed_resources`, and
`missing_resources`; the shadow sidecar remains an index and does not replace
canonical evidence storage.
