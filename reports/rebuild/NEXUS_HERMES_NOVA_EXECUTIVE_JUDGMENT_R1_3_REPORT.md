## Research State Semantics

Heartbeat liveness, scheduler enablement, process configuration, DRY_RUN,
task processing, queue state, and recent activity are independent fields. An
active heartbeat does not claim live task processing; an enabled scheduler does
not claim a currently executing task; DRY_RUN does not mean the control plane is
dead.
