# Hermes Nova evidence provenance model

Search results are discovery evidence. Retrieved page content is direct page
evidence. Nexus and Alpha results remain governed by their existing adapters and
provenance. The shadow evidence state records required resources, executed
resources, supported/partial claims, source quality, and currentness.

Prior conversational context is not evidence provenance. Reusable evidence must
carry its source turn/resource/result identity and remain valid for the current
objective. Currentness is `CURRENT`, `RECENT_BUT_NOT_CURRENT`, `UNDATED`,
`STALE`, or `UNKNOWN`; unknown freshness is not rendered as strong current
verification.

No authority, TruthKernel, cost, privacy, Nexus, or Alpha lifecycle boundary
was changed.
