# WP9D authorization concierge

The capability prepares existing-credential/session/OAuth discovery results,
least-privilege scope review, cost review, official authorization URL, and a
resume step. Password, MFA, passkey, OAuth consent, terms, account creation,
payment, and broad permissions remain irreducible Ray gates.

The implementation creates no account and does not bypass OAuth. It records a
Figma read/private-test-frame-write checkpoint with status
`WAITING_HUMAN_CONSENT`.
