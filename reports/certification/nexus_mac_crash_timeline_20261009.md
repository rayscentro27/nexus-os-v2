## Evidence caveats

- Unified-log and `pmset` queries returned no bounded pre-crash event detail sufficient to establish a power-loss or sleep/wake cause. Current power/space/swap readings are normal; they cannot reconstruct historical pressure.
- The supplied Supabase API-edge observations are not available as a complete local access log. Table rows identify the Mac host, process IDs, workflow actor, and denied provider attempts, but original HTTP response-byte totals and all remote client IP evidence were not returned by the read-only table projections.
- Five completed durable-job rows in the bounded campaign window did not have a matching durable-worker footprint row. No job was active at the reboot, but those missing receipts/footprints and the Marketing timestamp discrepancy require review before certification.
- Repository inspection found pre-existing modified files in the canonical checkout. They were left untouched; this report is the only file created by this audit.
