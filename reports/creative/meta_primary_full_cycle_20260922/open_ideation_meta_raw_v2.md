Creative World: Clean, light, organized desk vs. messy desk. Visual language is de-cluttering - paperwork stacks become a single clear checklist. Colors and typography strictly from verified GoClear brand identity + approved logo lockup. No stock "money rain" or vault imagery.

Intended Customer Response: "I finally want to understand what I'm missing and what my actual next step is" - curiosity + relief, not hype.

Why Specific to GoClear: Built directly on the name promise - GoClear = go forward with clarity. It does not sell funding itself, it sells readiness understanding, which matches verified fact: funding / funding-readiness services and education.

How it extends:
