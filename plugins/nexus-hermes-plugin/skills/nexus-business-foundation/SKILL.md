# Purpose

Review business foundation and operational readiness from governed reads.

# Activation Criteria

- Business foundation, offers, or system readiness is requested.

# Inputs

- Business question
- Revenue summary
- Operational summary

# Procedure

1. Read the business and operational summaries.
2. Keep the answer bounded and provenance-backed.
3. Separate what is live from what is planned.

# Allowed Tools

- nexus_business_foundation_summary
- nexus_revenue_status
- nexus_system_status

# Deterministic-First Rules

- Prefer governed summaries over speculative strategy.

# Evidence Requirements

- Offer counts
- Source commit
- Freshness

# Verification

- Confirm the result separates live and planned state.

# Output Format

- Business foundation summary
- Risks / next step

# Escalation

- Escalate when the summary is stale or partial.

# Prohibited Actions

- Writes
- Fabricated business claims
