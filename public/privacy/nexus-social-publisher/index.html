Nexus Social Publisher Privacy Policy

Effective November 13, 2026 · Applies to Nexus Social Publisher's TikTok integration. Facebook and Instagram are covered by the Nexus Social Integration Privacy Policy.

1. Who we are

Nexus Social Publisher is operated by GoClear ("GoClear", "we", "us"), the operator of the Nexus operations platform at goclearonline.cc.

2. Information we access from TikTok

We access a TikTok account only after its owner explicitly authorizes the app through TikTok Login Kit. Depending on the permissions granted, this may include:

  • Basic profile information: open ID, display name and avatar (user.info.basic).
  • Profile information such as your username and profile link (user.info.profile).
  • Permission to upload videos to your TikTok inbox as drafts for you to review and post (video.upload).
  • Access and refresh tokens issued by TikTok so the app can act on the authorized account.

We do not access your messages, followers or contacts, or any account you have not authorized.

3. How we use it

  • Identifying and connecting the authorized TikTok account.
  • Preparing content and, when you request it, sending videos to your TikTok inbox as drafts.
  • Showing connection and upload status.
  • Operating, securing and troubleshooting the service.

We use TikTok data only for these purposes and in line with TikTok's Developer Terms.

4. Sharing and sale

We do not sell, rent or trade TikTok user data. We do not use it for advertising profiles or share it with data brokers. Data is processed only by GoClear and by the infrastructure providers that host our services on our behalf, under confidentiality and security obligations. We may disclose information if required by law.

5. Security

Tokens are stored in encrypted, access-controlled credential storage and are never published, embedded in web pages or shared with third parties. Connections use encrypted transport (HTTPS/TLS), and access is limited to the services that need it.

6. Retention

We keep connection data and tokens only while the account stays connected. Content and status records are kept only as long as needed for content management, and no longer than 24 months. When access is revoked or deletion is requested, we delete the related tokens and account data within 30 days, unless the law requires us to keep it.

7. Revoking access

In the TikTok app, go to Profile → Menu → Settings and privacy → Security and permissions → Apps and services permissions, select Nexus Social Publisher and remove access. After that, the app can no longer reach your account.

8. Requesting deletion

Email support@goclearonline.cc with the subject "Data Deletion Request" and include your TikTok username. We will confirm the request, delete the related data within 30 days, and tell you when it's done.
