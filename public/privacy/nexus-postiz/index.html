Nexus Social Integration Privacy Policy

Effective November 13, 2026 · Applies to the Nexus social publishing integration (including its Postiz-based scheduler) with Facebook and Instagram.

1. Who we are

This integration is operated by GoClear ("GoClear", "we", "us"), the operator of the Nexus operations platform at goclearonline.cc. Nexus is used to manage social media content for businesses operated by GoClear's owner. It is not offered as a public sign-up service.

2. Information we access

Nexus only accesses a Facebook Page or Instagram professional account after the account owner explicitly authorizes it through Meta's login and permission screens. Depending on the permissions granted, this may include:

  • Basic profile information of the authorizing user (name and Meta user ID), as provided by public_profile.
  • The list of Pages and linked Instagram professional accounts the owner chooses to connect, with their names, IDs and profile images.
  • Content we publish or schedule on those accounts, plus related post metadata such as status, timestamps and links.
  • Engagement and insights data for those accounts and posts, such as reach, impressions, reactions and comments.
  • Access tokens issued by Meta that allow Nexus to act on the authorized accounts.

We do not access personal messages, friends lists, or accounts the owner has not selected.

3. How we use it

  • Connecting and identifying the authorized Pages and Instagram accounts.
  • Creating, managing, scheduling and publishing content the owner has approved.
  • Reading engagement and insights data to report on content performance.
  • Responding to and managing comments on content we published, where permitted.
  • Operating, securing and troubleshooting the Nexus and Postiz services that perform these tasks.

Publishing requires the owner's approval within Nexus. We use Meta data only for these purposes and in line with Meta's Platform Terms.

4. Sharing and sale

We do not sell, rent or trade Meta user data. We do not use it for advertising profiles or share it with data brokers. Data is processed only by GoClear and by the infrastructure providers that host our services on our behalf, under confidentiality and security obligations. We may disclose information if required by law.

5. Credential and token security

Meta access tokens and app credentials are stored in encrypted, access-controlled credential storage. They are never published, embedded in public web pages, or shared with third parties. Access is limited to the services that need it to perform the functions above, and connections use encrypted transport (HTTPS/TLS).

6. Retention and deletion

We keep account connection data and tokens only while the integration remains authorized. Post and insights records are kept only as long as needed for content management and reporting, and no longer than 24 months. When authorization is revoked or deletion is requested, we delete the associated tokens and account data within 30 days, except where retention is required by law.

7. Revoking Meta authorization

  1. Facebook: go to Settings & privacy → Settings → Business integrations (or Apps and websites), select the Nexus app, and choose Remove.
  2. Instagram: go to Settings → Website permissions → Apps and websites, then remove the Nexus app. For accounts connected through a Facebook Page, removing the app on Facebook also revokes access.

Once access is revoked, Nexus can no longer reach the account.

8. Requesting deletion

To ask us to delete data obtained through this integration, follow the Data Deletion Instructions or email support@goclearonline.cc.
