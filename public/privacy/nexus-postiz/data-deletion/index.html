User Data Deletion

Data Deletion Instructions

Applies to data obtained through the GoClear Nexus social integration with Facebook and Instagram. See the Privacy Policy.

Option 1: Remove the app from your Meta account

  1. Facebook: open Settings & privacy → Settings → Business integrations (or Apps and websites), find the Nexus app, and select Remove.
  2. Instagram: open Settings → Website permissions → Apps and websites and remove the Nexus app.

Removing the app revokes Nexus's access right away. We then delete the stored tokens and account data for that connection within 30 days.

Option 2: Email a deletion request

Email support@goclearonline.cc with the subject "Data Deletion Request" and include:

  • The name of your Facebook Page or Instagram account (or your Meta user ID).
  • A short confirmation that you want your data deleted.

We will confirm your request, delete the access tokens, account connection details, and stored post and insights records tied to your account within 30 days, and reply to confirm when it's done. We may keep only what the law requires.
