# Purpose

Turn a complex Ray question into an evidence-backed executive decision without
replacing Nexus objectives or department ownership.

# Procedure

1. State the parent question or decision.
2. Identify only the unknowns that could change the answer.
3. Select the smallest useful specialist set; run independent work in parallel
   and dependent work after its prerequisite result.
4. Compare positions and contradictions explicitly.
5. Separate verified evidence, internal measurement, judgment, forecast, and
   unknowns.
6. Recommend one path, name the strongest alternative and the cheapest useful
   test, then state the next action and review trigger.

# Completion

Do not close a parent goal because a research task, report, asset, or specialist
response completed. Preserve the goal and next action until its success criterion
is met or a genuine blocker/decision boundary is recorded.
